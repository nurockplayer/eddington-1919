# Independent re-measurement of r04 performance (Opus 5.5, 2026-10-20)

**Setup:**
- Builds `014e84d` (v0.1), `16dbbf6` (r03) and `61772a7` (r04 merged), each benchmarked with the **same** current `tests/benchmark.gd` via `art/pipeline/baseline.sh`.
- 3 repetitions per build, seed 7, 1600×900, Apple M2 Pro, Godot 4.7.2.
- Table of medians: [SUMMARY.md](SUMMARY.md), generated by `art/pipeline/perf_summary.py`.

**Metrics, kept separate:**
- **Draw calls and primitives:** what is submitted. These were identical in every repetition.
- **Wall-clock frame avg/p95:** display-capped at about 8.3 ms (120 Hz) even with vsync off.
- **forced-draw avg/p95:** `RenderingServer.force_draw(false)`, which renders without presenting. This is the uncapped render cost.
- **Not available:** GPU timestamps (`viewport_get_measured_render_time_gpu`) read 0 on this Metal driver.
- **Did not help:** render-scale stress (`--bench-stress`) is clamped at 2.0 and stayed under the cap.

## Findings
1. **Geometry: confirmed.**
   - Primitives fell 42–73% in 6 of 7 bookmarks.
   - Draw calls fell 5–18% in 6 of 7.
   - `totality_sky` went up: +17% draw calls and +30% primitives, because the shelter frame and rolled canvas now enter the upward view.
2. **Uncapped render cost: no measurable improvement.**
   - forced-draw averages are 2.4–3.3 ms in every build, and the differences sit inside the spread between repetitions.
   - One exception: `totality_sky` is r03 1.90 → r04 2.37 ms, outside the spread. It is small, and consistent with finding 1.
   - **The r04 geometry reduction did not make rendering faster on this machine.** At 2–3 ms per frame the scene was never geometry-bound. "Fewer primitives" must not be reported as an FPS gain.
3. **Wall-clock p95: unchanged** at about 9–10 ms across builds.
   - A first pass showed r04 p95 around 13 ms. Repetitions 2–3 of that pass overlapped Codex image-generation jobs (manifest timestamps 18:58–19:00 UTC vs run files at 18:58–19:00 UTC).
   - Those repetitions were discarded and re-run with nothing else active. **Rule: never benchmark while generation or other GPU jobs run.**
4. **Headroom:** with about 2–3 ms of uncapped cost against an 8.3 ms frame, there is about 5 ms per frame available for lighting quality (r05). The forced-draw figure becomes the G4 cost gate.
