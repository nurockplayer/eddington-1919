## Expedition dress (context)
- The only 1919 expedition group photograph found is Sobral's (BJHS 2025 Fig. 2). It shows pale drill and linen lounge suits, some dark suits, collars and ties, and straw boaters or panamas held in hand. **No pith helmets.**
- r06 therefore changes Eddington's v0.1 pith helmet to a straw hat. A sun helmet stays plausible, but it is less supported.
- Eddington's Quakerism concerned belief, not costume. He wears ordinary Edwardian dress in his portraits.
