## 1. Ranked proposal

Build toward two distinct payoffs: the expedition earns its encounter with totality; the observer earns a defensible change of mind afterward. Transfer the Owner's WoW reference as preparation, responsibility, anticipation and release, without importing combat, loot, raid choreography or assets. Awe must never certify the scientific verdict.

The arc is **trust → predict → doubt → test → revise** (`CONVERSION_ARC.md:8–14`). All player-feeling statements below are design inferences, **unconfirmed by a new human playtest**. Source evidence confirms the described behavior or missing capability, not enjoyment. F rows are targets, not fresh passes.

Already present: the Newton/Mercury investigation, prediction ledger, hand-operated box and light sketch, micrometer, four-way verdict, chart prediction marks, Sobral comparison and verdict recall. See `FUN_PLAN.md:22`, `CONVERSION_ARC.md:29–37`, and `reference_proposals_2026-11-17.md:12–15` (the latter two paths are under `docs/design/` and `docs/research/` respectively). Do not rebuild them.

No generated deflection or verdict is shown. Live seed, gaps, plates, inventory and totality clocks are identical before/after practice; pause freezes practice; original player position, roof and input return correctly. No practice result predicts the next live cloud gap.
- **Size / areas / risk:** **1 run**; rehearsal in `main.gd`, director-approved practice feedback in `content.gd`, translations and `tests/test_totality.gd`. Medium risk to practice timing/wording expectations; no alteration of live exposure physics or grading.
