## The feeling to leave with

**The relief of finding that you can change your mind and still belong.**

Kit shares the last sugar and helps check a star chart that implicates someone who cannot answer for it. The assistant wants a place at the bench and a piece of work worth bringing home. Ashby has good reasons to trust Newton and puts his own figure on the same page as the assistant's. He also gives the assistant the means to contradict him. Kit leaves a page for whatever the assistant actually sees. At Sundy those two kinds of trust come due together. The assistant may have crossed an ocean to bring back a blank plate. They still owe Dyson a defensible report and Kit an honest account of the sky. Another team's work can justify a change their own plates cannot. At the end, the old figure is still legible, the assistant can say why their view changed or what they are waiting for, and someone has kept a chair for them.

Give the player three different releases. At second contact they feel the scale of the sky against a person at an instrument. At the darkroom table they recognize what their own small marks can bear. In London the combined work becomes public and answerable. Keep Kit's answer small enough to hear after all that. A player who began at 0.87″ should be able to explain what earned their trust in the larger deflection. A player whose own plates failed can reach that same understanding through the independent result, and can still reserve judgment. A player who started at 1.75″ can leave with a reason they have actually tested.
