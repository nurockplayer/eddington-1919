# Sobral · Two instruments, one eclipse

Script-ready English copy for [The expedition journey](../JOURNEY.md). Sobral, Ceará, May–July 1919. Moves: none. Wire after the Príncipe slice is accepted.

## Entry and staging

The player works through a **documented-only reconstruction**, handling simulated plates and comparison choices from the observer's side of the table. They have no named role in the expedition. The Cambridge assistant was at Sundy in the fictional story, and the Sobral record identifies the people who operated, timed and supported the instruments. This framing gives the player the two technical discoveries without moving the assistant, adding a protagonist, or giving a real person invented actions or dialogue. Use attributed record text in place of acted speech. Each numbered row is one visible English string. Provenance is for production. The archive, simulation and date labels are visible to the player.
