# Fictional arcs across the journey

[Owner decision, #316](https://github.com/nurockplayer/eddington-1919/issues/316#issuecomment-7182266341) · Moves: none

Every character beat below is **INVENTED-FICTIONAL**. On the full journey, the assistant and Kit have two letters that cross and a third opened in person. Ashby’s trust in Newton has a professional cost when someone prints his old figure without its qualification. Edmund Rook, a fictional Cambridge colleague who writes opinion pieces, turns wartime tests of loyalty on the people doing the work. His private dialogue and labelled opinion sheet stay outside the hearing extracts, meeting record and real clippings. Apply the [historical boundaries](JOURNEY.md#lines-we-do-not-cross) throughout.

## Shipped dialogue to reconcile

In [October’s `kit_reunion()`](../../scripts/cambridge/content_epilogue.gd#L57), close Kit says the letter’s contents can wait until another day. Warm Kit says they have already said everything in it. O01/O02 replace those endings on the journey route. Keep the six-week explanation for the second, unsent letter and the existing Cambridge news. The reply to the sky promise concerns notes about changes at home. The Sundy letter is a separate earlier letter. Keep the opening line chosen at Sundy and use it before the envelope exchange. The prologue, M2 and slice coda still support these beats as shipped.
