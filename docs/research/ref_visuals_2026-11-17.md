## Answer

**Research cutoff: 2026-11-17 23:00 JST**, verified live; this supersedes the mistaken interim timestamps. Production accounts below describe historical workflows; shader licences and hardware figures reflect pages retrieved at this cutoff.

**INFERENCE:** The most practical route is surface-attached hatching, deliberately controlled outlines, and separate treatment for scientific evidence. A convincing result also needs composition and selective detail; the shader alone cannot settle those decisions for the art director.

### 1. Findings per reference

- **Return of the Obra Dinn — useful additional technical detail.** Pope’s camera-centred, world-oriented sphere mapping stabilises dither during **rotation, not translation**. His earlier edge pass combines position and face normals, with vertex-colour control to suppress unwanted edges. Separate lighting and texture ramps preserve legibility. **INFERENCE:** borrow the control over where lines appear; sphere mapping alone does not meet Eddington’s surface-stability requirement. Sources: [dither devlog](https://dukope.com/devlogs/obra-dinn/tig-32/), [edge/rendering devlog](https://dukope.com/devlogs/obra-dinn/tig-01/).

- **Tonal art maps — the strongest hatching foundation.** Praun, Hoppe, Webb and Finkelstein precompute nested hatch textures across tone levels and mip levels. Darker tones retain lighter tones’ strokes; coordinated scales preserve apparent stroke density during viewing changes. Their surface parameterisation follows a curvature field. **INFERENCE:** adopt the nested-tone principle first; procedural meshes can supply simpler, explicitly authored hatch directions before attempting the paper’s full parameterisation. Source: [paper, explanation and demo](https://hhoppe.com/proj/hatching/).

- **Stroke-based rendering and newer stability demos.** Hertzmann and Zorin generate silhouettes and hatch marks that convey surface shape. Runevision’s **Dither3D** instead keeps dots attached to surfaces while approximately maintaining their screen spacing by adding/removing dots. It is a Unity reference implementation under **MPL-2.0**, not an engraving solution or verified Godot port. **INFERENCE:** useful technical alternatives, but neither warrants building a general stroke engine first. Sources: [Illustrating Smooth Surfaces](https://mrl.cs.nyu.edu/publications/illustrating-smooth/), [Dither3D](https://github.com/runevision/Dither3D).

- **Mundaun — drawings genuinely attached to 3D.** Michel Ziegler modelled and unwrapped objects, printed the UV layouts, pencilled them, scanned them and reapplied the textures. He describes drawing as the means of unifying disparate source material. **INFERENCE:** transferable mapping workflow; substantial bespoke drawing remains a production cost. Fixed textures also do not themselves provide light-driven hatch density. Source: [creator interview](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2021/02/interview_how_one_man_is_making_mundaun_ps5s_first_horror_game).

- **Pentiment — constrain staging, then finish illustration.** Hannah Kennedy describes rough scene sketches used during narrative iteration, restricted movement tracks, controlled camera orientation and concentrating finish on important characters. **INFERENCE:** settle playable framing before producing final portraits/backgrounds. This is evidence for a production method, not a real-time 3D engraving technique. Source: [art director’s pipeline account](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/art/deep-dive-the-art-of-pentiment).

- **Inkulinati — make the medium part of the interaction.** Its developers describe a game played on manuscript pages, with characters, objects and battlefields grounded in marginalia and researched with a medievalist. **INFERENCE:** period diagrams can become usable interfaces rather than decorative overlays. The source does not establish a reusable 3D hatching pipeline. Source: [developer release account](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2024/02/22/inkulinati-1-0-is-now-available-to-play/).

- **Sable — additional readability lesson.** Shedworks describes distance fog, shadows and outlines whose opacity fades with distance as tools for spatial comprehension and hiding object appearance. **INFERENCE:** distant Príncipe vegetation should lose hatch contrast and detail; equal-strength linework everywhere would undermine depth. Sources: [developer GDC session](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1027721/The-Art-of-Sable-Imperfection), [talk coverage](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/marketing/how-shedworks-refined-the-art-of-sable-in-pursuit-of-readability).

- **Tchia — economical materials and skies.** Awaceb describes triplanar materials reducing UV/baking work, a skydome with painted clouds, one shadow-casting directional light, and curves driving material colours through the day. **INFERENCE:** a controlled sky and lighting sequence can deliver atmosphere without bespoke volumetric simulation. These are developer-reported production techniques, not measured Godot performance results. Source: [technical interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/tchia-is-a-beautiful-ambitious-undertaking-from-a-small-indie-studio).

- **Trüberbrook — physical craft plus restricted viewpoints.** Its environments originated as miniature sets digitised through photogrammetry. Its character designer explicitly prioritised shapes, silhouettes and gestures because the camera offered no close-ups. **INFERENCE:** the silhouette lesson transfers directly; building and scanning physical sets is poorly matched to your stated tools. Sources: [studio account](https://btf.de/en/truberbrook/), [character-design interview](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=757300&enddate=1552565440).

- **The Last Night — presentation reference, not shipping proof.** Odd Tales describes pixel art combined with modern lighting; its Steam page still says “To be announced.” **INFERENCE:** useful evidence for hybrid compositing, insufficient evidence that a tiny team can finish that production scope. Sources: [developer reveal](https://oddtales.net/blog/2017-the-last-night-reveal-trailer-at-e3-2017), [live Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/612400/The_Last_Night/).

- **Cocoon and Hob — establish a visual vocabulary.** Cocoon’s developers discuss colour-coded worlds and framing the necessary information in the first arrival screen. Hob’s director describes recurring visual cues for interactable objects. Cocoon’s rendering talk also documents froxel fog and volumetric lightmaps. **INFERENCE:** clarity is transferable; that volumetric stack is a separate engineering investment. Sources: [Cocoon interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/mental-staircases-and-paradoxical-suitcases-crafting-the-world-hopping-puzzles-of-cocoon), [rendering talk](https://gdcvault.com/play/1034619/Pondering-Orbs-The-Rendering-and), [Hob developer account](https://blog.playstation.com/2016/04/28/hob-designing-a-transforming-world-on-ps4/).

- **GRIS / Valiant Hearts — composition and emotional progression.** GRIS simplified its protagonist for extreme zoom-outs and animation economy, and planned a colour script tied to narrative progression. Valiant Hearts’ developer diary addresses art and emotion through illustrated presentation. **INFERENCE:** simplify distant figures and plan the entire value sequence. GRIS supports staged colour progression, not specifically an abrupt climax inversion. Sources: [GRIS developer interview](https://www.theverge.com/2018/12/20/18148805/gris-nintendo-switch-steam-art-animation), [Valiant Hearts developer diary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A28PRELsBnA).

### 2. Proposals for Eddington 1919

**All proposals, costs and risks below are INFERENCE.**

| Proposal | Player experience and affected part | Evidence and implementation | Cost / main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Build one moving engraving scene** | Walk around the coelostat, tilt toward clouds, approach timber and cloth; observe lighting change. Affects the shared environment material. | Use nested hatch tones driven by illumination. Give procedural meshes hatch UVs; use object-local projection where appropriate. Compare rotation, translation and approach separately. TAMs support coherent tone/scale changes; Pope demonstrates why rotation-only stability is insufficient. | **Medium.** UV seams, triplanar blending and distant aliasing may reveal the construction. |
| **2. Give evidence a quieter visual treatment** | Move from the engraved scene into a photographic plate, crisp micrometer and readable uncertainty plot. Affects measurement and the ledger. | Exclude evidence imagery and text from world hatching/palette processing. Keep instrument silhouettes distinct and their surroundings sparse. Apply Cocoon/Hob’s repeated visual cues without making “correct evidence” glow. | **Low–medium.** Visual emphasis could accidentally suggest an answer or obscure real measurement scatter. |
| **3. Script totality as a value event** | Repeated exposure tasks build anticipation; ambient sound thins, beige paper becomes blue, and white corona lines appear above small silhouettes. Affects the eclipse sequence. | Prepare a colour script: ordinary daylight → suppressed contrast → totality → recovery. Coordinate sky, paper, ink and lighting explicitly. Use a skydome, cloud layers and authored corona strokes initially. GRIS supports planned progression; Tchia supports economical sky control. Let music enter after a brief release of silence. | **Medium.** The palette change may flatten silhouettes or interfere with exposure timing. Spectacle must not certify relativity before measurement. |
| **4. Make a bounded asset-and-portrait pipeline** | Recognisable distant figures gesture in the scene; illustrated portraits carry dialogue and Ashby’s reaction. Affects characters and recurring locations. | Greybox framing first; assemble procedural architecture/props from a small kit with explicit UVs and edge controls. Reserve image-gen 2D for approved portraits, paper and selected surface accents. Keep provenance, source references and paintovers; typeset scientific labels separately in all four languages. Pentiment, Mundaun and Trüberbrook support different parts of this division. | **Medium.** Cross-image character consistency and material consistency may consume the saved modelling time. |
| **5. Establish a low-end rendering budget early** | The eclipse and measurement remain responsive and readable during motion. Affects renderer choice and effects. | Start with surface hatching, selective inverted-hull outlines and inexpensive fog. Compare against one depth-edge pass; add normal edges or volumetrics only after profiling. Propose 1080p/60 fps as a test target, with a below-modal GPU such as GTX 1650 as an additional test machine—not an established minimum. | **Low–medium initially.** Fine lines may shimmer, or effects may exceed the frame budget. No performance guarantee exists yet. |

### 3. Top pick

**INFERENCE: do proposal 1 first**, including one photographic evidence object and both paper palettes. It tests the largest shared uncertainty—whether the marks remain convincing while the player moves—before the team produces many assets. Deliver a short camera-motion capture and GPU timings for the art director’s assessment.
