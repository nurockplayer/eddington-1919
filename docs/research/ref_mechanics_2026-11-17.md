### 1. Findings

“Why it works” statements marked **INFERENCE** are transferable design interpretations, not experimentally established explanations of enjoyment.

- **Mars After Midnight:** Pope’s development account maps the crank directly to opening an inspection window; he preferred explicit D-pad looking over accelerometer control. **INFERENCE:** physical correspondence supplies tactility without requiring difficult motor control. [Developer devlog](https://dukope.itch.io/mars-after-midnight/devlog/495856/gameplay-loop).

- **The Signal State:** players manipulate modules and connect cables; cable colours and rendering styles are configurable. **INFERENCE:** giving an abstract relationship a manipulable physical representation makes reasoning inspectable—and revisable. [Official description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1577620/The_Signal_State/).

- **Strange Horticulture:** identification combines an encyclopedia with discovered clues; identified plants influence the story. **INFERENCE:** precision becomes worthwhile when an observation changes what someone can actually do, rather than merely filling a worksheet. [Official description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1574580/Strange_Horticulture/).

- **Unpacking:** its audio production addresses object/material interactions; its accessibility options include zoom, remapping, and operation without dragging or sustained holds. **INFERENCE:** responsive object sounds can deliver tactility while accessible inputs preserve it. [Audio-production account](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/marketing/auditory-tales-from-the-making-of-zen-puzzler-unpacking), [official accessibility details](https://www.unpackinggame.com/).

- **Viewfinder:** photographs reshape the environment, with rewind among the supporting mechanics. **INFERENCE:** a surprising consequence becomes inviting when players can inspect and undo their experiment. [Director’s account](https://blog.playstation.com/2023/07/18/reshape-reality-with-your-perspective-in-viewfinder-out-today/).

- **Superliminal:** forced perspective changes object scale; sound changes help communicate the resulting size. **INFERENCE:** coordinated visual and auditory consequences make an unfamiliar rule feel physically coherent. [Graphics programmer’s account](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=332742).

- **Shadows of Doubt:** its investigation board stores information and links evidence. **INFERENCE:** externalising relationships reduces memory work; however, a connection drawn by the player should remain distinguishable from an established fact. [Official description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/986130/Shadows_of_Doubt/).

- **Duskers:** remote microphones, drone views and motion sensors provide incomplete information; a motion signal does not identify its cause. **INFERENCE:** uncertainty is engaging when players understand what an instrument can—and cannot—observe. [Official description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/254320/Duskers/).

- **Iron Lung:** a grainy still camera, incomplete map and proximity sensors mediate observation. **INFERENCE:** operating an instrument and then waiting for limited evidence can make a modest image reveal consequential. [Official description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1846170/Iron_Lung/).

- **Observation:** players pair with systems before operating them, and reuse tools in different situations. **INFERENCE:** a short acquisition–adjustment–execution ritual establishes expertise; changing its purpose provides variation without adding more controls. [Creative-director interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/making-the-player-the-ai-in-outer-space-thriller-i-observation-i-).

- **Obra Dinn—only the confirmation rule:** fates are verified in groups of three; player accounts also describe guessing through remaining alternatives. **INFERENCE:** delayed validation builds anticipation, but does not eliminate brute force. Transfer the pacing, not an infallible scientific verdict. [Firsthand Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/653530/discussions/0/3049483485758530485/).

- **Hypnospace Outlaw:** investigations involve browsing websites, with optional hidden pages and secrets. **INFERENCE:** exploration becomes rewarding when apparently incidental material later becomes useful evidence. [Official description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/844590/Hypnospace_Outlaw/).

- **Type Help:** its mystery is reconstructed from files on an old computer. **INFERENCE:** an inexpensive archive can sustain investigation when new context changes the meaning of existing documents. [Creator’s page](https://william-rous.itch.io/type-help).

- **Botany Manor:** players associate clues with plants and experimentally establish growing conditions. Its accessibility documentation provides adjustable crosshairs, camera sensitivity, plain-text overlays and untimed puzzles. **INFERENCE:** the final bloom rewards the whole reasoning chain; readable instruments need not sacrifice atmosphere. [Firsthand review](https://access-ability.uk/2024/04/08/botany-manor-accessibility-review-switch/), [publisher documentation](https://whitethorngames.com/botanymanor/accessibility).

- **Exo One:** gravity manipulation and transformations preserve momentum across terrain and clouds. **INFERENCE:** anticipation, accumulation and release can make a simple action feel expansive—a useful pacing principle for totality. [Valve developer interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rx8kOIfHI4w).

**Educational simulations and learning research**

- **PhET:** its implicit-scaffolding framework uses affordances, constraints, cues and feedback to support exploration and ownership. **INFERENCE:** show an inviting control and a visible consequence before presenting terminology. [Research paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.6544).

- **Foldit:** direct manipulation and computational tools enabled top players to solve difficult protein-refinement problems. This establishes scientific problem-solving performance, not general educational transfer. **INFERENCE:** expose useful operations and consequences rather than translating every action into a question. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2956414/).

- **Eclipse simulators:** NASA’s Eclipse Explorer synchronises a time slider, moving shadows and a local eclipse view. **INFERENCE:** linking overview and local observation is particularly transferable to exposure planning. Its documentation does not establish learning effectiveness. [NASA documentation](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/skywatching/eclipses/solar-eclipses/2023-solar-eclipse/introducing-the-2023-eclipse-explorer-your-interactive-guide-to-the-2023-annular-solar-eclipse/).

- **Productive failure:** a meta-analysis of 53 studies found a moderate advantage for problem-solving before instruction, with important moderators. **Desirable difficulty** concerns later learning despite harder initial practice. **INFERENCE:** challenge the explanation, while teaching instrument operation clearly. [Meta-analysis](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.3102/00346543211019105), [Bjork laboratory](https://bjorklab.psych.ucla.edu/research/).

- **Predict–observe–explain:** a quasi-experimental study of 120 students found better conceptual understanding and game performance with POE guidance. **INFERENCE:** preserve the prediction and explicitly revisit the discrepancy; prediction alone is insufficient evidence for learning. [Primary-study abstract](https://scholars.uky.edu/en/publications/inquiry-enhanced-digital-game-based-learning-effects-on-secondary/).

- **Uncertainty:** hypothetical-outcome animations improved judgments involving comparisons between quantities, but generally offered similar accuracy for single-quantity questions. **INFERENCE:** try simulated repeat outcomes alongside labelled intervals, rather than assuming animation universally solves comprehension. [Controlled study](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0142444).

### 2. Proposals for Eddington 1919

**All proposals, costs and expected benefits below are INFERENCE**, based on your supplied scope.

1. **A micrometer with a satisfying recording ritual.**  
   **Player:** seats the photographic plate, opens a stationary loupe, makes coarse then fine adjustments, and locks a reading. Wheel ticks, moving graduations and an optional controller pulse acknowledge movement—not proximity to the “correct” answer.  
   **Changes:** darkroom measurement.  
   **Why:** Mars’ control correspondence, Unpacking’s material feedback, Observation’s tool ritual.  
   **Cost:** medium; existing instrument UI, a magnified crop and a small sound set.  
   **Risk:** repetitive labour. Offer keyboard nudges, adjustable sensitivity/zoom, toggle grip, larger crosshairs and no measurement timer. Avoid mandatory dragging.

2. **Let uncertainty grow visibly from the readings.**  
   **Player:** sees individual readings accumulate as dots, then a separate interval for inferred deflection. A labelled “simulated repeat experiments” view shows alternative outcomes; a static equivalent remains available.  
   **Changes:** scatter, error bars and the 2σ question.  
   **Why:** PhET’s linked representations and uncertainty-visualisation research.  
   **Cost:** medium; procedural charts and explanatory text.  
   **Risk:** false precision. Distinguish reading scatter, estimate uncertainty and shared plate effects. Approximately 95% coverage at twice standard uncertainty requires assumptions; small samples may need another factor. [NIST’s archival guidance](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297/nist-tn-1297-6-expanded-uncertainty).

3. **Make the ledger a forecast the player can stand behind.**  
   **Player:** chooses 0 / 0.87″ / 1.75″, previews the corresponding predicted displacement, and selects a reason. They seal the page. Predictions return as overlays only after readings are recorded; revisions appear beside the original entry.  
   **Changes:** prediction ledger and Ashby’s responses.  
   **Why:** POE and delayed confirmation give commitment a future consequence.  
   **Cost:** low–medium; ledger states, overlays and dialogue.  
   **Risk:** embarrassment becoming punishment. Ashby should respect warranted uncertainty and careful revision; choosing relativity earns no correctness bonus.

4. **Make exposure timing a rhythm of anticipation and release.**  
   **Player:** rehearses with linked sky and shutter views, then watches cloud movement and chooses when to expose. At totality, allow a brief unobstructed view of the immense sky before instrument work resumes.  
   **Changes:** cloud exposures and the epic climax.  
   **Why:** NASA’s linked views, Iron Lung’s delayed observation, Exo One’s accumulation/release principle.  
   **Cost:** medium; existing sky, cloud and audio sequencing.  
   **Risk:** weather feeling arbitrary. Make cloud motion readable and offer slower timing or an assisted shutter following the player’s chosen rule.

5. **Turn the box into a repeatable physical prediction.**  
   **Player:** places an expected impact marker, releases the ball, inspects its trace, then predicts the light trial. They can change one condition and repeat, with the previous prediction still visible.  
   **Changes:** thought-experiment box.  
   **Why:** PhET, Viewfinder and POE favour manipulable consequences and reversible exploration.  
   **Cost:** low–medium; procedural traces and markers.  
   **Risk:** treating a programmed demonstration as independent empirical proof. Present it as a model whose implications the eclipse observations must test.

6. **Give Sobral the reward of a second look.**  
   **Player:** reopens their original plate, forecast and uncertainty beside the arriving results, then points Ashby toward the comparison that changes—or fails to change—their conclusion. Preserve disagreement and ambiguous evidence.  
   **Changes:** epilogue and replay value.  
   **Why:** evidence boards and archive investigations make old material useful under new context.  
   **Cost:** medium; reuse existing objects with comparison UI and dialogue.  
   **Risk:** cherry-picking. Keep acquisition conditions attached and distinguish independent corroboration from repeatedly measuring one plate.

### 3. Top pick

**Build proposal 1 first, carrying its recorded readings directly into proposal 2.** It tests the smallest essential loop: *adjust → record → compare → reconsider*. If that feels satisfying without a correctness chime, the scientific persuasion has a strong mechanical foundation.
