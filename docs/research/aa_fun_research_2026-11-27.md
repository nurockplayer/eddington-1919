## Answer

The strongest fit for Eddington 1919 is the pleasure of **making an observation, acting on it, and seeing what it changed**. The closest comparisons make evidence worth looking at twice, give tools a response players can learn, and let a result carry the story forward. An eclipse plate can do all three: the player prepares the exposure, sees what they captured, and later uses that same plate to decide what the expedition can claim.

### 2. What feels AA on a small budget

- **Interaction and camera.** Acknowledge input as soon as it registers, then let a short eased motion settle into a clear end state. Give a detent, a latch, and a shutter distinct sounds and optional pad pulses. Keep the camera steady while the player is judging fine alignment, and provide coarse and fine adjustment on a pad. *Eddington: put the most care into seating the plate, releasing the shutter, and turning the micrometer.* [GDC, *Juice It or Lose It*](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1016487/juice-it-or-lose), [Xbox input guidelines](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/107)
- **Onboarding, focus, and saves.** Teach a single safe action through a visible result, then add one variable. Use consistent button prompts, strong focus outlines, readable captions, and a shape or label alongside color cues. Make Continue predictable and checkpoint at boundaries that preserve the player’s work. Prioritize usable text sizing, separate volume controls, remapping, and an adjustment for timing pressure where needed. *Eddington: make Cambridge the rehearsal, check focus and text fit in all four languages, and preserve the prepared setup when restarting totality.* [Xbox Accessibility Guidelines](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/guidelines), [SEASON quality-of-life patch notes](https://steamcommunity.com/app/695330/allnews/)
- **Audio.** Record a few variations for the sounds heard most often and distinguish materials where the player can hear the difference. Lower competing ambience under speech or a critical cue. Keep room tone or wind audible during concentration, and bring music in when a result lands. *Eddington: make cloth, glass, metal, and paper identifiable by ear and leave the shutter audible at totality.* [GDC, *Unpacking* foley](https://gdcvault.com/play/1027728/-Unpacking-The-Fun-Behind), [SEASON audio-team interview](https://www.asoundeffect.com/season-game-audio/), [Firewatch review](https://www.pcgamer.com/firewatch-review/)
- **Presentation.** Carry one typographic and visual treatment from title to chapter transitions, notebook, and credits. A recurring image or sound can bridge scenes with little new art. Give credits readable pacing and working skip and return controls. *Eddington: use the same engraving treatment from title to telegram, and let the final plate carry into the credits.* [Simogo production retrospective](https://simogo.com/work/loreleiandthelasereyes/)
- **Performance and stability.** Profile frame-time spikes during actual interactions and transitions. Check the first tool use in an exported build, a full pad-only path, and quitting and resuming at the pivotal checkpoint. A *Tchia* reviewer praised movement while a Steam player reported long startup and crashes on their setup. *Eddington: check that the first shutter press, darkroom transition, and resume into totality all respond smoothly on the weakest supported machine.* [Godot stable profiler docs](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/scripting/debug/the_profiler.html), [Steam player reports](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1496590/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated)

### 3. Where narrative science gets boring

| Friction | What the examples suggest | Application to Eddington 1919 |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading between actions** | A short document earns attention when it answers a question the player already has, as in *Golden Idol* and *Botany Manor*. | Put the relevant optics note beside the first plate that gives the player a reason to consult it. |
| **Being graded by the expert** | A later observation can confirm or overturn an idea, as in *Heaven’s Vault*, while *Pentiment* lets the player own a judgment under uncertainty. | Have Eddington ask which plate the assistant trusts and let the paired readings support the answer. |
| **Arbitrary failure** | *Viewfinder* makes the consequence visible and reversal quick. *Outer Wilds* lets knowledge survive a failed outing. | Show what a missed exposure cost on the actual plate and let the expedition proceed with whatever evidence the player captured. |
| **Slow walking** | *Firewatch* uses travel for navigation and conversation. The sailing complaints about *Heaven’s Vault* show what happens when transit repeatedly separates the player from the strongest verb. | Give a crossing one conversation the player can steer or one task to do, then cut to arrival. |
| **Unclear goals** | *Outer Wilds* gives players a log of leads and freedom over which to pursue. A useful objective names the unresolved question and keeps observations available. | Put the immediate question and last useful reading on the expedition log the player already uses. |

These are qualitative patterns from the comparisons. Habgood and Ainsworth’s educational-game experiments also support putting the subject matter inside the play mechanic, with evidence from a children’s math game. ### 4. Observation and measurement that feel good

- **Photography in *Umurangi Generation*.** Photo assignments make the player search for a vantage point and compose around a constraint. The scene becomes legible through looking, and the photograph records the player’s solution. *Eddington: give a trial exposure a visible target and let the resulting plate show the effect of the player’s setup.* [GameSpot review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/umurangi-generation-review-cyberpunk-snap/1900-6417524/)
- **Indirect instruments in *Iron Lung*.** Coordinates, heading, map, and snapshots provide different pieces of the same answer. Alternating between the tools creates a rhythm and makes a successful fix feel earned. *Eddington: let the player locate a reference star and center it on the reticle before reading the micrometer scale.* [Game review](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3707036/iron-lung-review/), [developer’s Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1846170)
- **Experimentation in *Botany Manor*.** Clues suggest a condition to reproduce, controls let the player reproduce it, and the growing plant makes the result unmistakable. *Eddington: let players take a second reading and see whether the two marks agree before committing the measurement.* [The Guardian review](https://www.theguardian.com/games/2024/apr/10/botany-manor-games-review)
- **Pressure in *Outer Wilds*.** Environmental windows become actionable as the player learns what changes and prepares the next attempt. The lesson for a scripted eclipse is to teach the sequence before the clock bears down. *Eddington: rehearse the plate change in Cambridge, signal the end of totality clearly, and let a missed shot affect the evidence in the darkroom.* [PC Gamer review](https://www.pcgamer.com/outer-wilds-review/), [GDC design talk](https://gdconf.com/article/attend-gdc-and-learn-how-outer-wilds-nailed-curiosity-driven-game-design/)

- **Show the verb early and throughout the first trailer.** Valve says Steam users look for gameplay, and Steam generates its microtrailer by sampling the first visible video across its length. Screenshots can carry the same promise by showing readable tools, evidence, and interface at play scale. *Eddington: open on a plate being loaded and exposed, then show the developed result, a micrometer adjustment, and the telegram decision.* [Steamworks trailers](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/trailer), [store-page review criteria](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/review_process)

Public demo data offers a rough engagement benchmark. Zukowski’s self-selected sample reported a median playtime of fourteen minutes for linear demos, and the commercial comparison used estimated revenue. *Eddington: use Cambridge to give players one complete observation and a question they want the expedition to answer.* [Steamworks demos](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/application/demos), [demo playtime survey](https://howtomarketagame.com/2022/10/26/what-is-a-good-median-play-time-for-a-demo-benchmark/)
