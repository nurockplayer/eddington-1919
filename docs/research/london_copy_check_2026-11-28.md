### Facts the shipped chapter omits

- **§1** Thomson gave the floor to Dyson, then Crommelin, then Eddington. The scene skips Crommelin’s explanation of the blur and his thanks to Brazilian collaborators Henrique Morize and Leocádio Araújo. [Sponsel](https://alistairsponsel.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/sponsel-2002.pdf), [Simões, Soares and Carolino](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007087425000305)
- **§1** Eddington described two usable Príncipe plates with five measurable stars apiece and suggested that cloud may have kept the mirror cooler. [Meeting extracts](https://revistas.usp.br/khronos/article/download/155595/155701), [Sponsel](https://alistairsponsel.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/sponsel-2002.pdf)
- **§1** Thomson called it the most important result for gravitation since Newton and cited Mercury’s perihelion. Alfred Fowler urged independent confirmation and mentioned Evershed’s clouded-out attempt to photograph Regulus. [Stanley](https://doi.org/10.1086/376099), [Kennefick, ch. 11](https://dokumen.pub/no-shadow-of-a-doubt-the-1919-eclipse-that-confirmed-einsteins-theory-of-relativity-9780691190051.html)
- **§1** Lindemann argued against solar refraction. Silberstein accepted the observed deflection, pointed to Newton’s portrait while urging caution, and argued that spectroscopy offered a more accessible test than another favourable eclipse. [Earman and Glymour](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/philosophy/docs/glymour/relativityeclipses.pdf), [Sponsel](https://alistairsponsel.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/sponsel-2002.pdf)
- **§1** Dyson called for checks at the next two eclipses, Eddington distinguished the law of gravitation from the wider theory, and Thomson closed by thanking Dyson and Eddington. [Kennefick, ch. 11](https://dokumen.pub/no-shadow-of-a-doubt-the-1919-eclipse-that-confirmed-einsteins-theory-of-relativity-9780691190051.html), [Sponsel](https://alistairsponsel.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/sponsel-2002.pdf)
- **§2** It was an afternoon meeting in the Royal Society’s crowded meeting room at Burlington House. Whitehead attended. The room measured roughly 45 × 36 ft, with pews on either side of a central aisle. [Sponsel, pp. 456, 460–461](https://alistairsponsel.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/sponsel-2002.pdf)
- **§2** Peter Chalmers Mitchell’s report ran at the upper right of page 12 on **7 November 1919 GMT**, alongside Armistice remembrance coverage. It compared the discovery to Neptune and acknowledged disagreement over its implications. The headlines are in the design, but the shipped route goes from [“Read the papers”](/Users/tachikoma/Developer/eddington-1919/locale/en.po:5369) straight to the [ending review](/Users/tachikoma/Developer/eddington-1919/scripts/cambridge/epilogue_director.gd:284). [*The Times* facsimile](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/science-maths-technology/the-restless-universe/content-section-1/?printable=1), [Mussell](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4213436/), [Sponsel](https://alistairsponsel.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/sponsel-2002.pdf)
- **§3** Dyson gave **0.97″ and 1.40″** aloud for the blurred Sobral plates. The chapter shows the published **0.93″ and 1.52″**. The quoted proceedings contradict Sponsel’s claim that the blurred series went unmentioned. [Earman and Glymour, p. 77](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/philosophy/docs/glymour/relativityeclipses.pdf), [expedition paper, p. 312](https://harksen.is/assets/files/eddington-grein.pdf)
- **§4** Einstein was absent and had already received preliminary word from Lorentz. The telegram appears in [the I4 design](/Users/tachikoma/Developer/eddington-1919/docs/design/journey/LONDON.md:246).
