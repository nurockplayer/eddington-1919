### 2. Proposals for Eddington 1919

**All proposals, timings and cost assessments below are INFERENCE.** Costs describe effort relative to your existing Godot, sampled-cue and ambience workflow.

1. **Build a playable “sound falls away” totality sequence.**  
   During cloud-timed exposures, daylight engraving cools into blue paper; selected daytime calls diminish, dusk insects emerge, and the musical pulse withdraws. Leave roughly two seconds of near-silence—wind, breath, apparatus—then introduce a broad, restrained cue beneath the white corona. Keep exposure controls active.  
   **Changes:** eclipse sequence. **Why:** Journey’s arrival/coda, WoW’s contrast, eclipse accounts. **Cost:** low–medium. **Risk:** music masks shutter timing or exaggerates ecological certainty; keep essential apparatus sounds clear.

2. **Commission one “prediction” motif with changing context.**  
   Establish a short piano phrase during Newton’s successful orrery demonstrations. Preserve its identity through the ledger, but leave its ending open during doubt and measurement. At the eventual evidence comparison, change its harmony and instrumentation rather than replacing it with a “relativity theme.”  
   **Changes:** prologue through epilogue. **Why:** Journey, Outer Wilds and Firewatch build meaning through recurrence. **Cost:** medium. **Risk:** an obvious triumphant variant can announce the answer before the player reasons.

3. **Give analysis its own earned musical arrival.**  
   Micrometer adjustment receives tactile sounds, with no warmer chord as the number approaches the expected value. After the player submits a comparison of prediction, measured shift and uncertainty, play a brief acknowledgment. Reserve fuller resolution for persuasive evidence and the later Sobral conversation; retain an open ending for ambiguous plates.  
   **Changes:** darkroom and epilogue. **Why:** Obra Dinn’s delayed confirmation, tempered by your uncertainty mechanics. **Cost:** low–medium. **Risk:** treating a statistical threshold as automatic truth; dialogue and music must reflect the actual conclusion.

4. **Use three simple Godot music states with optional stems.**  
   Implement preparation → totality → aftermath using `AudioStreamInteractive`; hold expandable preparation material until the eclipse event. Use `AudioStreamSynchronized` for an aligned pulse, sustained layer and melody. Keep `AudioStreamPlaylist` for gramophone selections. Trigger totality’s withdrawal immediately; use musical boundaries for less urgent transitions.  
   **Changes:** audio implementation. **Why:** Finch’s flexible timing and Gris’s event-shaped entrances; documented Godot capabilities below. **Cost:** low–medium. **Risk:** unequal stem lengths, awkward loops, or a bar-delayed corona entrance.

5. **Spend on identity and historical judgment.**  
   Seek a quote for four–six unique finished minutes: motif, preparation loop, totality cue and evidence coda, delivered with useful stems. Start with piano and bowed strings; consider one recorded soloist. Keep island music as a separately researched, situated human activity, with a paid Príncipe cultural collaborator.  
   **Changes:** soundtrack production and island scenes. **Why:** Heaven’s Vault’s intimacy, Pentiment’s specificity, KRZ’s performances. **Cost:** medium. **Risk:** presenting Portuguese or generic “African” music as interchangeable with Príncipe’s traditions.

[Suno’s commercial-rights explanation](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/9601665) distinguishes commercial permission from copyright protection. **INFERENCE:** preserve the applicable terms and generation provenance; a commissioned human motif is the clearer foundation for this small score.
