# Portrait provenance

`cottingham_neutral.jpg` and `cottingham_engraving.jpg` are generated reconstructions made with Codex’s built-in image generation. The engraving was redrawn from the generated print. No photograph of Cottingham was used or imitated. The dialogue UI labels reconstructed portraits “Reconstructed portrait, not a historical photograph”.

The prompts, source hashes and engraving tone adjustment are recorded in the two `cottingham_*.provenance.json` files.
